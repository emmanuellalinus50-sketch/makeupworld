ELLA MAKEUP — Luxury Beauty Store Website Demo
Designed by LUXE DIGITAL STUDIO

OPENING THE WEBSITE
1. Extract the ZIP folder.
2. Open the ella_makeup_website folder.
3. Double-click index.html. It should open in your browser.
4. Use the navigation to browse pages, add products to the cart and test the demo checkout.
5. Open admin/index.html to test adding/removing sample products.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- Responsive luxury purple, champagne-gold, ivory and blush design
- Homepage, shop, cart, demo checkout, about, services, appointments, contact, blog, reviews, account, wishlist, FAQ, shipping, returns, privacy, terms, gallery, new arrivals, best sellers, press and care guide pages
- 24 sample products with illustrative NGN prices
- Browser-local demo cart and admin product management
- WhatsApp contact link
- LUXE DIGITAL STUDIO credit

IMPORTANT BEFORE REAL BUSINESS USE
This is a static front-end prototype, not a production-ready transactional store.
- Product prices, inventory, customer reviews, contact details and policies are sample content and must be checked/replaced.
- Checkout does not process payments or send orders to a business.
- Admin is not secured; localStorage is browser-specific and is not a database.
- Secure authentication, server-side product/order storage, payment gateway, inventory management, email notifications and booking confirmation need a backend integration.
- Some editorial photos load from Unsplash and require an internet connection. The site layout and emoji product placeholders work offline.
